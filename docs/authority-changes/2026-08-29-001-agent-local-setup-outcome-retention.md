# Retain the settled Agent-local setup outcome without retaining its research trail

## One decision

Decide whether each current authored candidate or representative keeps one
minimal durable Agent-local comparison boundary while the investigated
equipment universe, guide material, detailed calculations, and complete
countermodels remain transient review evidence.

## Context

The workbench currently persists final candidate identities and deterministic
representatives, while supporting requirements preserve the reason for those
outcomes at inconsistent levels of detail. The source-fact owner correctly
rejects candidate rationale, guide references, authoring receipts, and possible
future use as reasons to retain source structure, but its wording can also be
read to discard the smallest settled Agent-local boundary that a later content
extension or semantic review needs to distinguish a current candidate from its
nearest rejected alternative.

Discarding every local comparison boundary makes later authoring begin again
from current arrays or external guides. That allows current membership to
validate itself and encourages a catalogue, inspection graph, or runtime score
to compensate for the missing reason. Retaining the entire investigation would
create the opposite failure: duplicated equipment facts and a permanent
evidence registry unrelated to the consumer's small competitive choice set.

## Existing rule

- Owning Rule IDs: `SW-004`, `SW-008`, `SW-009`, `SF-001`, and `SF-002`
- Conflict: the product rules own authored candidates and representatives but
  do not state which part of their settled Agent-local comparison remains a
  durable authoring input. The source rules make external investigation
  ephemeral and explicitly reject candidate rationale as a source-retention
  reason, but do not distinguish an already-current bounded product outcome
  from the research trail that produced it. Current owners therefore cannot
  decide whether a requirement should keep no local boundary, the complete
  audit, or a smaller middle form.

## Proposed change

For each current authored candidate membership, compression, or deterministic
representative, retain the smallest Agent-local outcome needed to preserve its
material user decision and reversal boundary. That outcome consists only of:

- candidate or representative identity;
- the Agent outcome axis the choice realizes;
- a usable or unused source clause only when that distinction changes the
  comparison;
- the nearest comparator that establishes the admitted or compressed boundary;
- the finite setup opportunity that materially distinguishes the choice;
- why a same-direction alternative remains in a different acquisition or
  allocation role, or why it is compressed; and
- the contrary condition that removes the choice or changes the representative,
  together with its current lifecycle or visible consequence when applicable.

This settled boundary is Agent-centered product authoring, not a copied source
fact, UI explanation, runtime score, or evidence payload. Shared equipment
facts continue to own rank, Base ATK, advanced stat, passive values,
progression, activation, scopes, and compressed Setup copy. Do not duplicate
those facts or a simple derived total in the Agent-local outcome.

Keep every guide page, source quotation, complete investigated equipment list,
detailed calculation, worksheet, and full countermodel transient. Do not
persist every rejected alternative; retain only the nearest comparator that
defines a current boundary. This decision does not require one file per Agent,
one physical schema, a candidate catalogue, or a semantic evidence registry.

## Evidence

- `src/workbench/content/engines.ts` (`ENGINE_IDS_BY_AGENT_AND_POOL`) records
  current W-Engine identities and derives availability subsets, but the arrays
  do not preserve the Agent-local comparison that admitted or compressed an
  identity.
- `src/workbench/content/representatives.ts`
  (`REPRESENTATIVE_SETUP_BY_AGENT_AND_POOL`) records deterministic complete
  first choices, but an identity and zero-substat package alone do not preserve
  the finite future opportunity or reversing case that justified the choice.
- `docs/brainstorms/2026-08-12-yidhari-manato-vertical-requirements.md`
  retains candidate identities, realized usable and unused axes, pool-specific
  representatives, finite Disc and substat opportunity, and rejected nearby
  alternatives without copying the shared W-Engine value tables. It
  demonstrates the bounded middle form already consumed by current authoring.
- `docs/solutions/workflow-issues/prevent-secondary-requirements-from-validating-themselves-2026-08-12.md`
  records repeated cases where implementation, tests, and reviews agreed with
  unsupported candidate or representative requirements. It identifies current
  arrays and zero-substat output as insufficient authoring proof, but remains a
  workflow learning rather than product authority.
- Accepted `ACR-2026-08-28-001` establishes that a common Agent relationship
  proves usable value but not competitiveness. The input-specific complete
  package, finite opportunity, comparator, and reversal boundary therefore
  remain necessary to explain a settled current product outcome.

## Nearest current consumer

Manato's current W-Engine and Disc authoring is the nearest established
consumer. His supporting requirement keeps Grill O'Wisp as representative,
preserves limited alternatives only through their realized HP, CRIT, Fire, or
Sheer relationships, excludes nearby ATK suppliers through the finite
conversion opportunity, and records the full/non-limited and direct-edit
consequences. Production consumes the resulting identities and representative;
the requirement keeps only the local boundary needed to review or extend that
current outcome without copying the equipment source values.

## Contrast

The complete set of legal Rupture W-Engines, the external guide pages inspected
for Manato, every remote alternative, and the arithmetic used to test them are
not current product outcomes. Persisting them would turn one bounded Agent
decision into an equipment catalogue and make stale research appear
authoritative. Shared W-Engine values provide the other contrast: they remain
durable because Setup and Result consume them directly, but they belong once in
the shared equipment fact rather than in Manato's local comparison boundary.

## Impact

- Permanent owners affected: `docs/setup-workbench-product-contract.md` must
  own the minimal settled Agent-local authoring outcome;
  `docs/source-fact-boundary.md` must distinguish that product outcome from a
  source fact or research-retention reason.
- Supporting requirements affected: when a current candidate or representative
  is added, removed, compressed, or re-audited, its owning Agent requirement
  keeps only the settled local boundary above and removes copied equipment
  facts or exhaustive research.
- Production and tests affected: no immediate runtime or test change follows.
  A later bounded authoring refactor may colocate candidate identities with the
  minimal local outcome, but it must not add a runtime score, explanation
  payload, equipment catalogue, or item-specific regression suite.
- Visible Setup or Result consequence: none directly. Setup continues to show
  the complete source-owned package and Result continues to project only
  relationships consumed by the current Agent and context. The retained local
  boundary protects the correctness of future candidate and representative
  changes rather than becoming consumer copy.
