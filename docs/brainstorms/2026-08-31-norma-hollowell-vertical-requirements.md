# Norma Hollowell Vertical Requirements

## Summary

Add Norma and Chief Sidekick through the current Agent-centered setup and
source-owned equipment flows. Preserve Norma's low-field Stun operation, her
initial-CRIT relationships, her party outcomes, and her conversion of received
Sheer Force into self ATK without turning Sheer Force into a general stat or
adding a rotation model.

---

## Product Flows

1. Applying Norma prepares a complete M0 setup for the selected availability
   pool with zero supplied substats. Full starts on Chief Sidekick and
   non-limited starts on Hellfire Gears; both start on King of the Summit,
   Woodpecker Electro, CRIT Rate / Fire DMG / Energy Regen.
2. Users may edit the bounded competitive W-Engine, Disc, main-stat, and
   effective-substat choices. Setup shows each selected equipment package from
   shared facts, including clauses that the selected holder cannot project into
   Result.
3. Result shows Norma's current initial-CRIT outputs, Daze actions, received
   Sheer Force and converted ATK, Additional Ability outcomes, exact selected
   equipment effects, and retained Mindscape differences. It does not show raw
   damage, raw Daze, Preheated Chamber, or rotation timing.
4. Party Apply, target-only pool or Mindscape preparation, direct edit,
   invalid-selection clearing, re-selection, zero-substat initialization, and
   incomplete selection continue through the shared lifecycle.
