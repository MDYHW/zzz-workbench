# Yanagi And Alice Bounded Anomaly Expansion Requirements

## Summary

Add Yanagi and Alice as the first rolling unit of the remaining through-2.8
Anomaly damage/buildup track. Both are Focus-eligible anomaly damage dealers
with independently triggered Polarity outcomes, but their retained setup and
Result meanings remain distinct: Yanagi amplifies Disorder and Electric
buildup, while Alice converts excess AM to AP and amplifies Physical anomaly
and Disorder outcomes.

The permanent owners are `SF-001`-`SF-004`, `GV-001`-`GV-003` and
`GV-005`-`GV-009`, `FM-001`-`FM-004` and `FM-006`-`FM-011`,
`SW-002`-`SW-016`, and `UI-001`-`UI-004`. This requirement settles only the
bounded Yanagi/Alice outcomes below. It does not restate those common policies
or authorize later Anomaly damage/buildup Agents.

---

## Problem Frame

The landed Anomaly verticals already establish AP/AM composition, ordinary and
action-scoped anomaly modifiers, provider delivery, source-stated operations,
threshold gauges, competitive equipment authoring, and shared preparation
lifecycle. Yanagi and Alice are the first remaining pair whose defining source
text also describes a new anomaly result as a percentage of an original
Disorder or Assault. Reading that prose as a final-damage instruction would
cross the permanent Result boundary, while excluding every related clause
would lose real current stat, modifier, provider, and coefficient-operation
consumers.

Their equipment inspection has the same risk. Several Anomaly W-Engines are
legally selectable and numerically positive while repeating AP, AM, buildup,
or ATK already supplied through the finite Slot 4/5/6 and substat opportunity.
Availability and positivity therefore cannot decide membership without the
holder interval, complete usable package, nearest same-axis comparator, and
prepared consequence.

---

## Key Flows

- F1. Prepared Yanagi or Alice
  - **Trigger:** A party containing the Agent is applied, or that Agent's pool
    or Mindscape is rebuilt.
  - **Steps:** Author the pool-local competitive choices, prepare one complete
    package, apply current party pressure, initialize effective-substat counts
    at zero, reconcile validity, and calculate only when every selection is
    complete.
  - **Outcome:** Setup begins from a deterministic competitive package and
    Result exposes only admitted current relationships.
  - **Covered by:** R5-R8, R14-R20
- F2. Polarity-related Result
  - **Trigger:** Yanagi or Alice has a complete setup. Additional Ability and
    Mindscape qualification selectively enable only their governed
    relationships.
  - **Steps:** Compose local selected sources, evaluate Alice's one-way
    AM-to-AP relation once, deliver ordinary providers and applicable
    coefficient operations or modifiers, and project canonical anomaly
    outcomes.
  - **Outcome:** The user sees exact stat, modifier, provider, gauge, and
    operation consequences without a raw Polarity coefficient or final damage.
  - **Covered by:** R2-R5, R10-R14, R18

## Requirements

**Yanagi identity and retained relationships**

- R1. Add Tsukishiro Yanagi as S-Rank Electric Anomaly, Section 6, M0 by
  default, and Focus-eligible. Her completed level-60/full-Core values are ATK
  872, AP 114, and AM 148; no current Potential Awakening supplies another
  qualifying outcome. Her primary setup and Result families are
  `anomaly_damage` and `anomaly_buildup`. She supports both sustained on-field
  and shorter Polarity entries, but those intervals do not currently produce
  different competitive packages.
- R2. Yanagi's two stance buffs are compatible in the Fully Enabled snapshot:
  Jougen supplies Electric DMG +10% and Kagen supplies PEN Ratio +10%, because
  the previous stance persists after a switch. Completed Core supplies Yanagi
  another Electric DMG +20% against the EX-hit target. Her Additional Ability
  qualifies with another Anomaly or Electric Agent and supplies Electric
  Anomaly Buildup +45% after the retained stance-switch Basic route. Do not
  expose stance identity, timer, Basic string, or Shinrabanshou state.
- R3. Completed Core also supplies all-party Disorder DMG Multiplier +250%
  during the reachable EX window. Preserve this as an all-party source-stated
  coefficient operation for compatible `anomaly_damage` recipients and the
  canonical Disorder outcome. Polarity Disorder's 15% of original Disorder
  plus AP-dependent component is instead part of its anomaly base result; do
  not expose the raw percentage, AP skill coefficient, original-Disorder
  input, or final damage.
- R4. Retain only Yanagi Mindscape outcomes with current consumers. M1 adds
  AP +80 while at least one reachable Clarity stack exists; stack count and
  invulnerability remain invisible. M2 adds Electric buildup +20% to the EX
  rapid thrust. M4 makes attacks against the Exposed target gain PEN Ratio
  +16%, delivered from the enemy context to compatible current formula
  recipients. M6 adds EX Special Attack DMG +20%. M2/M6 changes to the
  Polarity original-Disorder percentage remain excluded base-result
  coefficients, and M3/M5 ordinary skill levels add no retained output.

  threshold gauge. Four AP counts activate the engine's Disorder bonus. M1
  activates it at zero counts through visible AP +80 while Clarity stays
  invisible. Polarity base percentages never appear.

  Core coefficient operation reaches Alice's compatible Disorder outcome.

  AM-to-AP relation without removing her local Core or equipment effects.
- AE3. **Covers R5-R8.** Yanagi full prepares
  Timeweaver/Chaos/Freedom/AP/PEN/AM; non-limited prepares
  Weeping/Chaos/Freedom/AP/PEN/AM. Broad pre-PEN pressure changes PEN to

  Practiced/Fanged/Phaethon/AP/PEN/AM; non-limited prepares

  It shows no Blade Etiquette, remaining-duration control, Polarized Assault

  Mindscape switch, selected pressure present/absent/reselected, direct edit,

- AE7. **Covers R21.** Original-asset inspection and browser comparisons cover

- AE8. **Covers R19-R20.** Pointer hover and keyboard focus on Yanagi's

## Success Criteria

- Users receive deterministic, competitive full and non-limited starting
  packages for Yanagi and Alice and can see how their selected investment
  changes admitted anomaly stats, modifiers, operations, and thresholds.
- Polarity source text is represented at the smallest current consumer:
  coefficient modifications remain visible while original-result arithmetic,
  local resources, cadence, and final damage remain absent.
- The implementation extends shared content and mechanism coverage without an
  Agent-specific calculator, runtime candidate scorer, or catalogue test suite.
- Yanagi and Alice close as one verified rolling unit without changing the
  boundary for later Anomaly damage/buildup Agents, Miyabi, or Anton/Rina.

## Scope Boundaries

- No final anomaly damage, buildup gauge progress, application history,
  remaining-time editor, rotation, uptime, field-time simulator, or resource
  model.
- No raw Polarity Disorder, Polarized Assault, Physical-anomaly DoT, or M6
  extra-hit coefficient Result.
- No universal Polarity abstraction, reaction registry, runtime equipment
  optimizer, rejected-item catalogue, or copied equipment facts inside an
  Agent profile.
- No Freedom Blues Result correction before the Anomaly damage/buildup track
  closes; it remains a competitive Setup choice only.
- No Vivian, Aria, Sunna, Nangong Yu, Promeia, Miyabi, Anton, Rina, or post-2.8
  production admission in this unit.
- No Agent-specific test suite when shared reference integrity, relationship,
  lifecycle, projection, and representative journey coverage can prove the
  behavior.

## Key Decisions

- Pair Yanagi and Alice first: their Polarity prose is the nearest semantic
  contrast, while their visible consumers prove different shared mechanisms.
- Retain coefficient changes but exclude original-result arithmetic: this
  follows the permanent anomaly-base and Result boundary rather than the kit's
  sentence structure.
- Keep candidate outcomes local: equal Specialty and positive arithmetic do
  not override complete-package competition or finite setup opportunity.
- Reuse one-way linear and provider-operation mechanisms: current consumers do
  not justify a new calculation stage or generic Polarity framework.
- Continue the Anomaly damage/buildup track through rolling semantic units: one giant requirements
  document would force unsettled later-Agent meanings into this implementation.
