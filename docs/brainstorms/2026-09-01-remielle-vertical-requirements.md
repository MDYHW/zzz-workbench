# Remielle Vertical Requirements

## Summary

Add Remielle, Ode of Resurrected Wings, and Feathered Fate through the current
Agent-centered setup and source-owned equipment flows. Preserve Lumiflux as
Remielle's declared Attribute while resolving her applied Attribute outcomes
from the cyclic next Agent in the applied party, including slot 3 to slot 1.
Keep Refringe as its own formula factor and Luminize as its own added multiplier
and exact Attribute Anomaly action. Do not turn either into ordinary Anomaly DMG
Bonus or automatically propagate Refringe into Abloom, Vortex, Luminize, or
other source-local anomaly outcomes. Present both independent numeric outcomes
under Anomaly Proficiency because AP is their shared visible basis.

## Product Flows

1. Applying Remielle prepares one complete M0 setup at zero supplied substats.
   Full starts on Ode of Resurrected Wings W1; non-limited starts on Weeping
   Gemini W5. Both use Feathered Fate 4-piece and Freedom Blues 2-piece. Full
   uses Anomaly Proficiency / ATK% / ATK% mains; non-limited uses ATK% / ATK% /
   ATK% mains. Both start with ATK%, Anomaly Proficiency, and flat ATK effective
   substats at zero.
2. Users may edit the bounded competitive W-Engine, Disc, main-stat, and
   effective-substat choices. Setup discloses Remielle's Lumiflux identity and
   each selected source package in compressed source-owned copy.
3. Result resolves Remielle's Attribute-dependent contributions from the next
   Agent in applied-party order. It shows Refringe and Luminize as separate
   exact action values nested under Anomaly Proficiency, Phase Flow party DMG,
   the capped squad ATK
   relationship, Prismatic buildup, stun-duration extension, and current
   Mindscape differences. Draft edits do not alter these values before Apply.
4. Party Apply, pool changes, and Remielle Mindscape changes reuse current
   rebuilding. Direct setup edits do not rerun preparation; incomplete setup
   remains incomplete and receives no fallback.

## Requirements

### Source, identity, and exact outcomes

- R1. Admit Remielle as an S-Rank Lumiflux Anomaly Agent in the Covenant of
  Dayat, with M0 as the default and off-field as her authored operating
  interval. Retain level-60 completed-Core base ATK 823, Anomaly Proficiency
  170, and no other completed base stat: these are the only current
  selected-source and Result stat consumers. Current released data contains no
  Remielle Potential Awakening entries, so full released Potential is the empty
  default. Core and Mindscape outcomes remain separate.
- R2. Lumiflux remains Remielle's declared Attribute for holder eligibility,
  setup identity, and source-owned Lumiflux clauses. When a Remielle effect
  requires an applied Attribute, it uses the declared Attribute of the next
  Agent in the applied three-slot party, with slot 3 wrapping to slot 1. Draft
  order does not affect this relationship until Party Apply. No generic
  same-Attribute inference, party sorting, or selected-Focus substitution is
  admitted.
- R3. Refringe uses a distinct factor equal to 0.02% per Fully Enabled Anomaly
  Proficiency. A complete three-Anomaly applied party adds 10%. This factor
  applies only to the exact Refringe action and does not enter ordinary Anomaly
  DMG Bonus. Coexisting anomaly actions do not inherit it: Abloom, Vortex,
  Luminize, Disorder, and other source-local outcomes require their own exact
  source relationship.
- R4. Luminize is an exact Attribute Anomaly child using the next applied
  Agent's Attribute. Its added multiplier is 0.2% per Fully Enabled Anomaly
  Proficiency. Ordinary Attribute Anomaly DMG Bonus may reach Luminize through
  that exact parent relationship; the added Luminize multiplier and Refringe
  factor remain separate. Retain Luminize reachability through Remielle's
  source actions without importing raw skill multipliers, raw or final damage,
  Voidflare history, or a cadence/rotation model.
- R5. Phase Flow supplies all-party DMG Bonus at 18%, 21%, or 24% according to
  the current M0/M3/M5 skill tier. Flower & Feather Dance extends Stun duration
  by 3s. These are separate current Result consequences; the stun extension is
  not normalized into Daze or a timing simulator.
- R6. Additional Ability activates when another applied Agent is Anomaly or
  shares Covenant of Dayat. It converts Initial ATK into capped squad flat ATK:
  6%, 12%, or 40% according to one, two, or three Anomaly Agents, capped at
  1600, and Prismatic targets receive 15% Anomaly Buildup. The ATK relationship
  exposes its current basis, cap, and output; it does not create a party-count
  editor. The source-stated Phase Flow Daze clause does not enter Result:
  Remielle's authored direction strengthens anomaly delivery rather than Daze,
  and formula validity alone does not create a Daze-contributor role.
- R7. M1 gives Luminize 50% all-Attribute RES Ignore and gives other party
  members 10% DMG to their own Attribute Anomaly. M2 adds 20% to the Refringe
  factor and gives Anomaly-specialty party members 15% DEF Ignore for Anomaly
  DMG against Prismatic targets. M4 adds 12% to the Luminize multiplier. M6
  records that Rainbow's End and Fleeting Grace trigger Luminize twice. M3/M5
  affect the Phase Flow skill tier only; they do not add a generic skill-level
  Result row.
