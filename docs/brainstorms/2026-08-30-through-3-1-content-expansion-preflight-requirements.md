# Through-3.1 Content Expansion Preflight Requirements

## Summary

Bound the Version 3.0 and 3.1 Agent and equipment cohort, distinguish reusable
content-extension meanings from genuinely new game concepts, and produce a
dependency-aware sequence of small verticals without admitting or implementing
the cohort during preflight.

---

## Problem Frame

The through-2.8 cohort is complete, and the current workbench now separates
shared equipment facts, ordinary effect materialization, Agent-local setup
policy, exact Agent outcome profiles, and visible Setup and Result projection.
That structure has been verified against the current roster, but it has not yet
been falsified by a later release that combines ordinary extensions with new
Attribute and Anomaly meanings.

Version 3.0 and 3.1 together introduce five Agents, six S-Rank or A-Rank
W-Engines, and four Drive Disc sets. Beginning with one convenient Agent could
discover a shared source or authority dependency only after an earlier vertical
has settled its local outcome. Preparing the entire later release in advance
would create the opposite failure: an equipment catalogue, speculative source
structure, or a batch of candidate decisions without current Agent consumers.

The current permanent owners do not yet define Wind or Lumiflux. Their presence
in the bounded cohort therefore cannot be treated as proof that they are a new
base Attribute, a special Attribute mapping, an existing anomaly result, or a
new formula family. The exact source relationships and current product
consequences must be reconstructed before dependent vertical planning begins.

---

## Key Flows

- F1. Bounded cohort reconstruction
  - **Trigger:** The through-2.8 expansion and setup content-extension refactor
    are present on trusted `main`.
  - **Steps:** Verify the official Version 3.0 and 3.1 release identities;
    separate Agent, W-Engine, and Drive Disc entities; apply the product's Rank
    boundary; and exclude every later identity.
  - **Outcome:** One finite inventory exists without admitting an Agent or
    equipment candidate.
  - **Covered by:** R1-R5
- F2. Source and semantic readiness
  - **Trigger:** The finite cohort is verified.
  - **Steps:** Decompose each retained source package; test ordinary effects
    against current outcome relationships and consumer joins; isolate new game
    concepts or operations; and inventory portrait and source readiness.
  - **Outcome:** Every proposed vertical has an explicit reuse, extension,
    authority-stop, or source-conflict classification.
  - **Covered by:** R6-R14
- F3. Rolling-vertical handoff
  - **Trigger:** Every cohort identity has a readiness classification.
  - **Steps:** Order small verticals by semantic dependency and contrast value,
    identify the first content-extension sentinel, and record the exact stop
    gates that later verticals must close.
  - **Outcome:** The first vertical can enter its own Agent-centered authoring
    without inventing cohort, equipment, authority, or sequence policy.
  - **Covered by:** R15-R18

---

## Requirements

**Authority and bounded admission scope**

- R1. Start from the exact trusted `main` revision and reconstruct current
  meaning from all five permanent owners, applicable current supporting
  requirements, the completed-milestone index, current source and calculation
  consumers, and the content-extension sentinels. Previous task conclusions,
  the completed Version-2.8 roadmap, current arrays, tests, and guides cannot
  supply a new product or semantic conclusion.
- R2. Bound the Agent cohort to the identities first introduced in Version 3.0
  and Version 3.1: Velina, Norma, Pyrois, Remielle, and Sigrid. Verify each
  identity's current released Rank, Attribute, Specialty, faction or party
  qualification, progression, and exact kit facts from current official
  sources. Exclude every identity first introduced after Version 3.1.
- R3. Bound the new W-Engine inventory to the S-Rank and A-Rank identities
  first introduced in those versions: Joyau Dore, Chief Sidekick, Sol Exuvia,
  Boisterous Echoes, Ode of Resurrected Wings, and Knight's Extolment. Exclude
  every B-Rank W-Engine under the existing service investment boundary and
  exclude later equipment.
- R4. Bound the new Drive Disc inventory to Wuthering Salon, The Sky Ablaze,
  Feathered Fate, and Thorned Rose. Preserve exact set identity and 2-piece and
  4-piece source ownership without treating release association as Agent value.
- R5. Keep inventory, source readiness, product admission, and implementation
  separate. A verified official identity or legal equipment package does not
  enter the current roster, candidate set, representative, Setup, or Result
  during preflight.

**Source and equipment readiness**

- R6. For every inventoried W-Engine, verify Rank and limited status, Base ATK,
  advanced stat, Rank-default refinement values, every passive clause,
  progression, activation, holder and Specialty eligibility, Attribute,
  affected action, recipient, interval, stack or cap, and compressed Setup
  consequence. Record only identity and readiness or gap outcomes in the
  preflight; do not copy a second permanent set of source values.
- R7. For every inventoried Drive Disc, verify its 2-piece and 4-piece clauses,
  activation, progression, holder, Attribute, action, recipient, interval,
  same-effect identity, and compressed Setup consequence. Inspect each clause
  independently before considering one complete set package.
- R8. Classify every new equipment clause as one of: expressible through the
  current ordinary relationship and applicability flow; requiring an already
  established unique operation; exposing an exact missing source or
  relationship edge; or introducing a genuinely new common concept. Equipment
  identity, trigger wording, or source association alone cannot justify a new
  handler or shared abstraction.
- R9. Use current guides only as discovery and interpretation input. For each
  credible guide, record its page-update date separately from the actual game
  build or calculation version it covers. Guide inclusion, omission, ordering,
  and disagreement neither admit nor reject a package.
- R10. When a later Agent could use an already admitted W-Engine or Drive Disc,
  inspect only the bounded nearest packages required to test the exact outcome
  relationship, complete-package competitiveness, acquisition or allocation
  role, and finite opportunity. Do not add existing equipment to the new-release
  inventory or rebuild a legal-item catalogue.
- R10a. Apply the reciprocal boundary when a vertical introduces a new
  W-Engine or Drive Disc. Reverse-route its independently classified source
  clauses through the current roster's existing holder, Attribute, action or
  operation, recipient, interval, formula, role, and current-consumer gates.
  Deeply compare only the conjunctive survivors and their nearest same-axis or
  same-role comparators, then record each affected Agent-local changed or
  no-change outcome in its current requirement. Guide lists and current
  candidate arrays are not the starting cohort, and the transient eliminated
  roster does not become an equipment catalogue.

**Agent and semantic readiness**

- R11. Derive each Agent's direction, roles, supported actions, formula
  participation, operating interval, basis or conversion relationships,
  delivery topology, role resources, external outcomes, focus eligibility, and
  exclusions from exact current facts and consumers. Attribute and Specialty
  qualify source clauses but do not create the direction or formula family.
- R12. Treat Wind and Lumiflux as unresolved named game concepts until current
  official facts establish their exact relationship to base Attributes,
  anomaly results, damage and buildup applicability, equipment scopes, and
  current visible choices or Results. If a required relationship is not owned
  by current permanent authority, identify the exact missing owner decision and
  stop dependent vertical work under `GOV-001` rather than mapping by analogy.
- R13. Apply `SF-005` separately to each proposed new term, value, condition,
  relation, and payload. Keep only distinctions that would change a current
  admitted candidate, prepared setup, editable choice, Result, or the policy or
  applicability needed to produce one after the bounded content is approved.
  Do not create release metadata, an admission registry, or a speculative
  capability axis.
- R14. For each proposed vertical, state whether an ordinary new W-Engine or
  Drive Disc can flow from shared source facts through current holder,
  Attribute, action, interval, recipient, and formula joins without an item-ID
  materialization branch. A failure must identify the exact new operation,
  missing fact grammar, consumer gap, or invalid current abstraction; it cannot
  be hidden in an Agent-specific exception merely to pass the sentinel.

**Vertical sequence and handoff**

- R15. Sequence later work by semantic dependency, reusable-mechanism contrast,
  exact party relationship, equipment dependency, and bounded risk rather than
  release phase, banner order, popularity, Specialty, or signature association.
  Prefer an existing-meaning Agent as the first content-extension sentinel only
  after its current source and consumer edges prove that classification.
- R16. Leave each Agent's exact W-Engine, Disc, main-stat, effective-substat,
  full-pool and non-limited representatives, and selected-input lifecycle to its
  own approved vertical. The preflight identifies comparison horizons and
  blockers but does not inherit another Agent's candidate or representative
  conclusion.
- R17. Inventory original portrait provenance and availability for all five
  Agents, but leave optical scale, registration, desktop and narrow rendering,
  expanded and compact acceptance, and any UI correction to the owning
  vertical. Source availability does not establish visual readiness.
- R18. End preflight with one advisory report containing the verified cohort,
  source and portrait readiness, ordinary-extension classifications, new
  semantic or operation gates, nearest established and contrasting consumers,
  proposed vertical order, first sentinel recommendation, and exact blockers.
  Obtain separate approval before writing a vertical requirement, plan, source
  fact, production code, test, or UI change.

## Acceptance Examples

- AE1. **Covers R2-R5.** If a current source lists a Version-3.2 Agent beside a
  Version-3.1 Agent, the former remains outside the cohort and no team conclusion
  depending on that later identity enters preflight.
- AE2. **Covers R3, R5.** If source data exposes a B-Rank W-Engine released in
  the bounded period, preflight records no candidate or shared-fact work for it
  because the service supports only admitted S-Rank and A-Rank W-Engines.
- AE3. **Covers R6, R8, R14.** If a new W-Engine supplies an established stat
  through an established activation, action, recipient, and formula scope, it
  is classified as an ordinary content extension. Its identity does not earn a
  new materialization branch.
- AE4. **Covers R7, R8, R14.** If one new Disc has two clauses in different
  effect families, preflight preserves both source clauses and tests their
  independent applicability before declaring the package ordinary or blocked.
- AE5. **Covers R9-R10, R16.** If a current guide omits a newly released engine
  or uses calculations from before its release, that silence opens a bounded
  omission comparison; it does not reject the package or expand the permanent
  inventory.
- AE6. **Covers R11-R13.** If Wind or Lumiflux can be named but its anomaly
  result or base-Attribute relationship would change formula or equipment
  applicability and current owners do not define that edge, the dependent
  vertical stops at the exact owner decision instead of reusing an Ice, Ether,
  or generic Anomaly relationship by analogy.
- AE7. **Covers R15-R18.** If Norma, Pyrois, or Sigrid is proven to use only
  established concepts while another earlier-release Agent requires a new
  Attribute decision, the established-concept Agent may become the first
  sentinel even when that differs from release order.

## Success Criteria

- The product owner can see the exact five-Agent, six-W-Engine, four-Disc scope
  and why B-Rank and post-3.1 content are absent.
- Every proposed vertical has an exact predecessor, contrast, source and
  portrait readiness state, content-extension classification, and explicit
  semantic or operation stop gate.
- The first vertical can begin Agent-centered candidate authoring without
  rediscovering the cohort or inventing a shared equipment or Attribute model.
- The preflight creates no current candidate, representative, runtime score,
  catalogue, speculative abstraction, or visible Setup or Result change.

---

## Scope Boundaries

- No Agent, W-Engine, or Drive Disc first introduced after Version 3.1.
- No B-Rank W-Engine.
- No Agent admission, candidate or representative conclusion, source-fact
  mutation, portrait calibration, implementation plan, production code, test,
  UI change, commit, push, or pull request as part of the preflight analysis.
- No exhaustive re-audit of already admitted Agents or equipment beyond the
  nearest current consumer, contrast, and bounded competitive comparator needed
  by one proposed vertical.
- No permanent release field, cohort registry, equipment catalogue, evidence
  registry, runtime optimizer, universal effect graph, or Named-Agent decision
  tree.
- No assumption that Wind, Lumiflux, a new anomaly result, or a new equipment
  operation fits an existing meaning because its label or outcome looks
  similar.
- No broad roster UI, color, layout, or portrait-polish pass. Each admitted
  vertical still owns its required portrait acceptance.

---

## Key Decisions

- Use one through-current Version-3.1 preflight and small later verticals: this
  exposes cross-release source dependencies without batching content admission.
- Inventory only S-Rank and A-Rank W-Engines: B-Rank remains outside the current
  service rather than becoming research or source-fact carrying cost.
- Inventory new equipment facts once and settle value per Agent later: shared
  source meaning is reusable, while candidates and representatives remain
  Agent-centered product outcomes.
- Prefer an established-meaning first sentinel but do not select it from
  identity metadata: exact current facts and consumers must prove that it
  exercises reuse rather than concealing a new concept.
- Treat Wind and Lumiflux as authority questions only where a retained current
  consequence requires their missing relationship. Their novelty does not
  justify a universal Attribute or reaction framework in advance.

---

## Dependencies / Assumptions

- The trusted `main` revision remains the starting checkpoint for preflight.
- Current official Version 3.0 and 3.1 sources remain available to verify
  identities, release scope, kit and equipment meaning, and current revisions.
- Two independent current build guides are sought for each Agent where credible;
  their absence is recorded as a discovery limitation rather than filled by
  speculation.
- The current ordinary equipment materialization and Agent-centered setup
  policy remain established consumers to falsify, not authorities that can
  validate a new meaning.
