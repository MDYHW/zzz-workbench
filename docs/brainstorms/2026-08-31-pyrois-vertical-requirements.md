# Pyrois Vertical Requirements

## Summary

Add Pyrois, Sol Exuvia, and The Sky Ablaze through the current Agent-centered
setup and source-owned equipment flows. The four Ultimate source forms were
inspected, but only current consumed relationships remain: Mirage's canonical
Ultimate and Sunflare's broad outcome. Omit the neutral form identities alongside
their raw damage. Preserve the qualifying Additional Ability and current Mindscape
differences without adding a resource, cadence, Contamination, or raw-damage model.

## Product Flows

1. Applying Pyrois prepares one complete M0 setup with Sol Exuvia W1, The Sky
   Ablaze 4-piece, Puffer Electro 2-piece, CRIT Rate / PEN Ratio / ATK% mains,
   and zero supplied substats in either availability pool.
2. Users may edit the bounded competitive W-Engine, Disc, main-stat, and
   effective-substat choices. Setup shows every selected source-owned package,
   including selected clauses whose exact holder cannot project them to Result.
3. Result presents one canonical Ultimate action for Mirage, Pyrois's retained
   broad Core and Additional outcomes, Mindscape differences, and selected
   equipment effects. The four source forms were inspected; form identity has no
   current consumer after the accepted omissions and is not retained for possible
   future use. Setup compression and Result projection stay independent.
4. Broad pre-PEN pressure removes Puffer Electro 2-piece and PEN Ratio Slot 5.
   Authorized preparation uses the authored pressure-safe package; direct edits
   clear only selections made invalid by the pressure and choose no fallback.
