# Ellen And Soukaku Vertical

## Summary

Admit Ellen and Soukaku from the initial Version-2.8 content cohort using their
current released behavior. Ellen is an Ice Attack Focus and general-damage
consumer. Soukaku is an A-Rank Ice Support buffer whose current Section 6
faction, rank-default Mindscape, Initial-ATK cap, single-recipient flat ATK,
squad Ice DMG, and enemy Ice RES Reduction reuse established typed party,
buffer-cap, recipient, and enemy-context mechanisms.

The vertical reuses current candidates, pool-specific representatives,
zero-supplied-substat preparation, contextual Puffer Electro, formula/action
applicability, completeness, rank, faction, and portrait behavior. It does not
add a rotation, resource simulator, raw damage, equipment optimizer, historical
version switch, named-party table, or guide-backed evidence payload.

## Product flows

- Applying Ellen and Soukaku prepares complete local setups, initializes all
  effective substat counts at zero, and calculates Result only when all three
  slots are complete.
- Their shared Ice identity activates both Additional Abilities without a
  named pair rule. Removing the qualifying relationship removes only the
  qualified effects.
- Soukaku's transferable Core buff is a user-steerable one-recipient effect.
  Canonical operation projects it once to the legally eligible Focus. It is not
  duplicated onto Soukaku or the entire squad.
- Full and non-limited pool changes rebuild only the changed Agent. Direct
  equipment edits preserve unrelated selections and never invoke hidden
  representative repair.
- Dialyn adds Ellen's existing contextual Puffer Electro 4-piece opportunity;
  it does not change Ellen's base candidate membership or automatically select
  the set.
