# Anton And Rina Bounded Shock-State General-Damage Expansion Requirements

## Summary

Add Anton Ivanov and Alexandrina Sebastiane as the bounded closure after
Miyabi. Their shared dependency is Shock state, not Specialty or personal
formula family. Anton is an Attack Agent whose personal actions and added
Shock operation remain `general_damage`. Rina is a Support Agent whose own
setup has no admitted personal damage formula, while her PEN-based Core and
Potential, Shock interaction, and Electric buffs deliver party outcomes to
compatible recipients. Neither relationship authorizes an `anomaly_damage`
profile for either Agent.

This unit applies `SF-002`-`SF-005`, `GV-001`-`GV-004` and `GV-009`,
`FM-001`-`FM-004` and `FM-006`-`FM-011`, `SW-002`-`SW-006`,
`SW-008`-`SW-016`, `SW-018`-`SW-020`, and `UI-001`-`UI-004`. It settles Anton
and Rina only. It does not reopen the
completed Anomaly-damage track, Miyabi, or existing candidate rosters.

## Requirements

- R1. Add Anton as A-Rank Electric Attack, Belobog Heavy Industries, M6 by
  default, and Focus-eligible. Retain completed level-60/full-Core ATK 791,
  CRIT Rate 19.4%, CRIT DMG 50%, and Energy Regen 1.2/s. His setup and Result
  formula family is `general_damage`. Shock is a condition and source-local

  Piledriver attacks and +40% for Drill attacks. Retain the qualified

  critical-hit trigger and cooldown do not become a rotation, counter, or
  final-damage consumer.

  history remain absent because they change no admitted setup choice or
  visible Result beyond the retained maximum.
