# Velina Airgid Vertical Requirements

## Summary

Add Velina Airgid, Joyau Dore, Boisterous Echoes, and Wuthering Salon through
the current Agent-centered setup and source-owned equipment flows. Preserve
Wind as its own Attribute, Windswept as its Attribute Anomaly, Vortex as a
separate anomaly result with its own coefficient operation, Contamination's
one-Attribute target consequence, exact Abloom actions, Initial Energy Regen
conversion, off-field delivery, Daze, party qualification, current Mindscapes,
and the absence of a released Potential Awakening. Do not reopen Pyrois or
Sigrid's previously deferred Contamination clauses in this bounded vertical.

## Product Flows

1. Applying Velina prepares one complete M0 setup at zero supplied substats.
   Full starts on Joyau Dore W1; non-limited starts on Weeping Gemini W5.
   Both use Wuthering Salon 4-piece, Swing Jazz 2-piece, Anomaly Proficiency /
   Wind DMG / Energy Regen mains, and AP and ATK% effective substats at zero.
2. Users may edit the bounded competitive W-Engine, Disc, main-stat, and
   effective-substat choices. Setup discloses each selected source package in
   compressed source-owned copy, including direct event Energy or routine
   trigger details that do not become a Result relationship.
3. Result shows the Initial Energy Regen conversion, Windswept and Vortex as
   distinct actions, Abloom under Windswept, Sweeping Cyclone Daze and buildup,
   Wind and Contamination-Attribute buildup RES reduction, and current
   Mindscape differences. Focus selects the controllable Contamination
   Attribute in Fully Enabled; only exact eligible current recipients project
   it, rather than forcing it onto Focus or exposing a new editor.
4. Party Apply, pool changes, and Velina Mindscape changes reuse current
   rebuilding. Direct setup edits do not rerun preparation; incomplete setup
   remains incomplete and receives no fallback.

## Requirements

### Source, identity, and exact outcomes

- R1. Admit Velina Airgid as an S-Rank Wind Anomaly Agent in the External
  Strategy Department, with M0 as the default and off-field as her authored
  operating interval. Retain level-60 completed-Core base ATK 872, Anomaly
  Proficiency 165, Anomaly Mastery 112, and base Energy Regen 1.2 because the
  current selected-source and Result consumers use them. Current released data
  contains no Velina Potential Awakening entries, so full released Potential is
  the empty default. Core and Mindscape outcomes remain separate and neither is
  relabeled as Potential.
- R2. When Initial Energy Regen exceeds 1.2, each additional 0.01 supplies
  0.21% DMG and 0.5 Anomaly Mastery, capped at 35% and 84 respectively. The
  joint cap basis is 2.88. Only percentage Energy Regen composes into this
  Initial basis. Automatic `/s` regeneration and direct event Energy grants do
  not enter it.
- R3. Windswept is Velina's Wind Attribute Anomaly and remains under the shared
  Attribute Anomaly action. Abloom is an exact child of Windswept. Vortex is a
  separate anomaly result: it occurs instead of Disorder only when a completed
  non-Wind Attribute Anomaly aligns with Contamination while Windswept is
  active. Vortex inherits neither Disorder nor generic Attribute Anomaly
  modifiers without exact source coverage.
- R4. Velina's completed Core retains Condensed Cyclone and Sweeping Cyclone as
  source routes. At two Windbite, the next Vortex gains +150% Vortex DMG
  Multiplier and summons Sweeping Cyclone. This is a Vortex base-coefficient
  operation, not an ordinary Anomaly DMG bonus. Condensed and Sweeping Cyclone
  dissipation trigger Abloom with fixed 145% and 255% Wind Anomaly coefficients;
  these establish the action and formula but raw base coefficients do not become
  editable or simulated final damage. Enhanced EX Special: Eye of the Storm and
  Ultimate each trigger the previous Agent's Quick Assist. Together they supply
  the existing repeated and external Quick Assist opportunities without a new
  recipient list, cadence model, or Astral-specific operation type.
- R5. The first Sweeping Cyclone hit on a Contamination target converts it to
  the corresponding Attribute and reduces Wind Anomaly Buildup RES by 7% for
  35s. A converted Cyclone also reduces the corresponding Attribute's buildup
  RES by 7%. Fully Enabled selects that one controllable corresponding
  Attribute through Focus; only exact eligible current recipients project it.
  Structurally ineligible Focus is not forced. Duration and refresh are
  compressed from Setup while the holder, target, Attribute, action, and Result
  consequences remain.
- R6. Additional Ability activates when another applied Agent is Anomaly or
  Wind. It increases Velina's Windswept and Vortex DMG by 10%, raises the two
  retained buildup-RES reductions to 14%, increases Sweeping Cyclone Daze by
  30%, and increases its Attribute Anomaly Buildup by 15%. Velina's Ultimate
  against a Wind Anomaly triggers exact Abloom at a fixed 680% coefficient; the
  exact Ultimate/Abloom action is retained while the raw base coefficient is
  intentionally omitted from Result calculation.
- R7. M1 adds 20% Sweeping Cyclone Daze, gives Velina's Vortex 20% all-Attribute
  RES Ignore, and gives squad Windswept 20% Wind RES Ignore. M2 lets Windswept
  grant Windbite, permits converted Cyclone to build the corresponding
  Attribute while excluding that buildup from Anomaly DMG calculation, and
  raises the Additional Windswept/Vortex bonus by 15% to 25%. M4 adds 15% ATK
  after EX Special. M6 retains Windbite recovery only as source reachability,
  adds 20% Wind buildup against a Wind Anomaly, and adds up to 40% Windswept
  DMG on reapplication. M3/M5 skill levels add no normalized Result row.
