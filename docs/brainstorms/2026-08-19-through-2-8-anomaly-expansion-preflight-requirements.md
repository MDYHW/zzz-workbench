# Through-2.8 Anomaly Expansion Preflight Requirements

## Summary

Define one bounded preflight that inventories the complete through-2.8 Anomaly
cohort and its direct setup enablers, closes a dependency-aware vertical order,
and separates reusable facts from Agent-local authoring before rolling vertical
expansion begins.

---

## Problem Frame

Grace is the only currently admitted Anomaly Agent and the first current
consumer of Anomaly Proficiency, Anomaly Mastery, anomaly damage, anomaly
buildup, Shock, and bounded Disorder qualification. The remaining work is not
limited to Agents with Anomaly Specialty. Anton, Rina, Yuzuha, Nangong Yu, and
other direct teammates can materially change qualification, party supply,
candidate pressure, prepared choices, or visible Results for Anomaly setups.

Starting one Agent at a time without a bounded inventory would repeat portrait
and equipment research, obscure dependency order, and risk discovering a
shared relationship after an earlier vertical has already settled its local
outcome. Preparing every remaining Agent, portrait, W-Engine, and Disc in
advance would instead create a catalogue of facts and copy without current
consumers. The preflight must find the narrow boundary between those failures.

---

## Key Flows

- F1. Cohort reconstruction
  - **Trigger:** The approved Grace vertical is stable and follow-on expansion
    is being considered.
  - **Steps:** Reconstruct the current landed roster, verify the official
    through-2.8 release boundary, identify every remaining Anomaly consumer and
    direct setup enabler, and exclude later content.
  - **Outcome:** One evidence-backed bounded cohort exists without admitting or
    implementing any new Agent.
  - **Covered by:** R1-R5
- F2. Shared-readiness inspection
  - **Trigger:** The bounded cohort is known.
  - **Steps:** Inventory portrait and equipment source readiness, classify
    reusable Grace mechanisms and new semantic demands, and preserve
    Agent-local candidate and copy decisions for their verticals.
  - **Outcome:** Reusable facts are ready to be consumed without creating an
    unowned catalogue or speculative framework.
  - **Covered by:** R9-R16
- F3. Rolling-vertical handoff
  - **Trigger:** Cohort and readiness inspections are complete.
  - **Steps:** Order basic then advanced verticals by semantic dependency and
    useful contrast, state blockers, and stop for advisory approval before any
    plan or implementation begins.
  - **Outcome:** The first bounded vertical can begin without inventing cohort,
    source, asset, or sequencing policy during planning.
  - **Covered by:** R6-R8, R17-R18

---

## Requirements

**Authority and bounded cohort**

- R1. Reconstruct current progress from the trusted current checkout,
  permanent owners, applicable current requirements, landed consumers, and
  behavior-bearing tests. Prior tasks, archived sessions, predecessor
  repositories, and the suspended roadmap cannot supply current meaning.
- R2. Verify the exact through-2.8 identity-admission boundary from current
  official sources. Exclude every entity first introduced after Version 2.8
  and every team conclusion that depends on that later entity, even when a
  current guide presents them together. For an eligible through-2.8 entity,
  use its current released identity, progression, kit, equipment facts, and
  values, including a later revision or progression extension when it changes
  a current qualifying outcome, as required by SF-001.
- R3. Inventory both remaining Anomaly consumers and non-Anomaly Agents whose
  exact party effect, qualification, equipment interaction, or common pairing
  can materially change an Anomaly setup or Result. The latter group includes,
  but is not limited to, Anton, Rina, Yuzuha, and Nangong Yu when current facts
  confirm the relationship.
- R4. For every inventoried Agent, distinguish official identity and retained
  facts from current product admission. An Agent absent from the landed roster,
  preparation, calculation, portrait, and visible consumers remains unadmitted
  regardless of roadmap placement or guide coverage.
- R5. Classify each primary consumer as a standard anomaly-damage/buildup case,
  an Agent-local threshold, conversion, Disorder, off-field, or reaction case,
  or a general-damage-led exception such as Miyabi when current kit evidence
  supports that direction.

**Vertical sequencing**

- R6. Re-evaluate the existing basic-cohort hypothesis of Anton + Rina, Piper,
  Yuzuha, Burnice, and Jane + Seth against current official facts and permanent
  owners. Preserve that order only where each vertical has an established
  predecessor, a useful contrast, and no unresolved earlier semantic
  dependency.
- R7. Keep advanced consumers and enablers such as Yanagi, Vivian, Alice,
  Miyabi, Aria, Sunna + Nangong Yu, and Promeia after the basic flow unless
  current evidence proves that one is a prerequisite rather than merely a
  later consumer.
- R8. Order verticals by semantic dependency, shared equipment and party
  relationships, contrast value, and bounded implementation risk. Release
  chronology, popularity, or a guide's team order cannot decide the sequence
  by itself.

**Portrait and equipment readiness**

- R9. Inventory original portrait availability, authoritative source,
  intended roster position, naming identity, and missing assets for the whole
  cohort before the first new vertical begins.
- R10. Leave portrait crop, face and head framing, scale, compact presentation,
  and desktop/narrow visual acceptance to the Agent's vertical. The preflight
  does not batch-calibrate or admit portraits.
- R11. Inventory one finite through-2.8 equipment universe once: every
  Anomaly-Specialty S-Rank and A-Rank W-Engine first introduced by Version 2.8,
  and every Drive Disc set first introduced by Version 2.8. Use each eligible
  item's current released facts and record rank, pool availability, Base ATK or
  fixed package supply, advanced stat, refinement progression, exact effect,
  trigger, recipient or holder restriction, condition, same-effect identity,
  and controllability. Exclude B-Rank W-Engines and every equipment identity
  first introduced after Version 2.8.
- R12. Keep common equipment facts separate from Agent-local competitive
  judgments. An inventoried item gains no candidate membership, prepared
  priority, or visible Result merely because another Agent consumes an
  equal-looking effect.
- R13. Final Setup compression remains consumer-specific. Each vertical must
  account for the complete package, unused clauses, partial limited
  competition, slot cost, condition reliability, and selected-versus-candidate
  parity before settling compressed copy.

**Harness and vertical entry gate**

- R14. Identify which Grace-established meanings can be reused directly:
  anomaly damage and buildup directions, AP and AM stat projection, broad
  pre-PEN pressure, pool-local representatives, zero supplied effective
  substats, candidate reconciliation, completeness, and Empty Result.
- R15. Identify every proposed new formula, reaction, party-delivery phase,
  candidate pressure, conversion, threshold, gauge, or Result operation before
  assigning its first vertical. Do not prebuild a generic Anomaly framework or
  broaden the calculation harness without a current bounded consumer.
- R16. Use AP-first as a rebuttable authoring premise for standard Anomaly
  consumers because AP supply is more limited than ATK supply within the
  conservative finite-investment boundary. It does not make an AP-labeled item
  beat a stronger complete package, replace Agent-local inspection, make eight
  effective-substat counts a cap, or apply to a general-damage-led exception.
  If this premise is to become permanent generic product meaning rather than a
  bounded authoring aid, stop for the authority transaction owned by GOV-001.
- R17. For each proposed vertical, record the nearest established consumer,
  contrasting consumer, exact source-to-visible chain, candidate and two-pool
  representative consequence, preparation and direct-edit lifecycle, portrait
  readiness, equipment readiness, and any semantic, external-fact, or product
  decision blocker.
- R18. End the preflight with an advisory cohort and ordering report. Do not
  create an implementation plan, admit content, add assets, change production
  code, or start the first vertical until the bounded order and blockers receive
  separate approval.

## Acceptance Examples

- AE1. **Covers R2-R4.** If a current guide discusses a through-2.8 Agent with a
  later teammate, the preflight uses the eligible Agent's current released
  behavior, excludes the later teammate and conclusions that depend on that
  teammate, and does not treat guide coverage or roadmap placement as landed
  admission.
- AE2. **Covers R3, R6.** Anton and Rina may enter the inventory even though
  they are not Anomaly Specialty when their current Shock or party effects
  materially change an established Grace consumer. Their candidate and Result
  outcomes remain unsettled until their bounded vertical.
- AE3. **Covers R6-R8.** Yuzuha does not open party AP scaling before at least
  two current contrasting Anomaly consumers can test recipient meaning and the
  cap, unless current evidence establishes a different prerequisite order.
- AE4. **Covers R9-R10.** A portrait with an authoritative original and known
  roster position is marked source-ready, but it is not marked visually ready
  until its own vertical completes desktop, narrow, expanded, and compact
  acceptance.
- AE5. **Covers R11-R13.** A W-Engine effect may be inventoried once, while one
  holder consumes its whole package and another loses an Attribute-only clause.
  The two verticals may therefore reach different membership, priority, and
  compressed-copy outcomes.
- AE6. **Covers R14-R16.** A standard Anomaly consumer begins with AP-first
  finite-investment reasoning, but a materially stronger ATK-forward complete
  package can still become its representative. Eight supplied-count checks do
  not claim AP saturation or a farming maximum.
- AE7. **Covers R15, R17-R18.** If a future Agent derives an outgoing party
  effect from an already received effect, the preflight reports the current
  phase-documentation blocker and does not hide it inside that Agent's plan or
  invent a solver.

---

## Success Criteria

- The product owner can see the complete bounded cohort, why related
  non-Anomaly Agents are included, and why post-2.8 content is absent.
- Every proposed vertical has a dependency-aware place, a current predecessor
  and contrast, and explicit source, asset, equipment, harness, and authority
  readiness.
- A later requirements author does not need to rediscover the roster boundary,
  repeat shared equipment research, guess portrait provenance, or invent the
  next vertical's semantic prerequisites.
- No production catalogue, simulator behavior, speculative abstraction, or
  unapproved Agent-local candidate outcome is created by the preflight.

---

## Scope Boundaries

- No Agent or equipment entity first introduced after Version 2.8; current
  released revisions of eligible through-2.8 entities remain in scope.
- No final anomaly damage, buildup gauge, application history, remaining-time,
  rotation, field-time, or team simulation.
- No mass Agent admission, portrait calibration, content mutation, plan,
  implementation, commit, push, or pull request.
- No permanent exhaustive Agent decision tree or universal candidate matrix.
- No inheritance of Grace's Thunder Metal, Timeweaver, or other local package
  outcome by a later Anomaly Agent.
- No previous-task, archived-session, predecessor-repository, guide-ranking, or
  roadmap conclusion as authority.

---

## Key Decisions

- Use a thin full-cohort preflight followed by rolling verticals: this reduces
  repeated research and sequencing drift without creating a consumerless
  catalogue.
- Inventory portrait provenance for the whole cohort but calibrate per
  vertical: source readiness is reusable while visual correctness is
  Agent-specific.
- Inventory equipment facts once but settle membership, representatives, and
  compression per consumer: fixed facts are reusable while competitive meaning
  is not.
- Treat the suspended roadmap as a hypothesis to revalidate: its dependency
  reasoning is useful, but it neither certifies current facts nor authorizes
  continuation.

---

## Dependencies / Assumptions

- The trusted current checkout remains the starting repository state for the
  preflight.
- Current official sources remain available to verify release version,
  identity, kit, and equipment facts; secondary guides may assist but cannot
  self-authorize a product outcome.
- Grace remains the only landed Anomaly consumer and the reusable baseline
  unless the trusted checkout changes before the preflight begins.
