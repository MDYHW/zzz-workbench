# Corin And Lycaon Vertical

## Summary

Admit Corin and Lycaon from the initial Version-2.8 content cohort using their
current released facts. Corin adds an A-Rank Physical Attack Focus with full and
non-limited whole-package representatives, a Dialyn-created contextual Puffer
Electro choice, Seed Vanguard participation, and current faction relationships.
Lycaon adds an S-Rank Ice Stun buffer and Daze contributor with completed
Potential Awakening, a local King of the Summit representative, a base Astral
Voice alternative, King-selected CRIT pressure, and bounded King/Astral holder
allocation beside Dialyn or Trigger.

The vertical reuses the established authored-candidate, preparation, direct-edit,
completeness, calculation, and Result flow. It does not introduce an equipment
optimizer, named-party registry, action simulator, evidence payload, or
Agent-specific framework.

---

## Problem Frame

The current workbench already distinguishes authored base candidates,
contextual candidates, selected-input-derived pressure, and prepared first
choices. It also owns shared formula applicability, exact equipment identity,
whole-package comparison, party preparation, non-stacking effects, and
incomplete-selection behavior. Corin and Lycaon must extend those mechanisms
without copying an abandoned candidate vertical or allowing its passing tests
to define product meaning.

Corin needs a pool-specific W-Engine decision that compares every usable and
unused clause together with the complete zero-substat Disc package. Steel
Cushion cannot become the full-pool default merely because it is her standard
S-Rank option. Lycaon needs exact holder eligibility before Disc comparison:
Moonlight Lullaby's stronger-looking retained numbers do not apply to a Stun
holder, while Astral Voice is usable through Lycaon's current Quick Assist
operation. Candidate dominance and Result recipient projection remain separate
decisions.

The accepted product behaviors also close two previously missing common
boundaries. Version 2.8 selects the initial entity cohort but does not freeze
those entities to historical Version-2.8 values. A user-steerable one-recipient
effect projects once to a legally eligible Focus; game-determined recipients
and genuine multi-recipient effects keep their actual rules.

## Actors

- A1. Setup workbench user: applies a party containing Corin or Lycaon,
  receives one complete prepared setup per Agent and pool, compares bounded
  candidates, edits current inputs, and reads the resulting Setup and Result.
- A2. Workbench session: resolves party, Focus, pool, Mindscape, preparation,
  effective candidates, holder allocation, selected-input pressure,
  completeness, recipient applicability, and synchronous Result recalculation.
- A3. Content author: keeps only current identity, setup, candidate,
  representative, calculation, and Result facts that pass the permanent
  retention gate.

## Key Flows

- F1. Corin or Lycaon party application
  - **Trigger:** The user applies a valid party containing either new Agent.
  - **Actors:** A1, A2
  - **Steps:** The session resolves Focus, prepares all three Agents from their
    current party context and pools, allocates any non-stacking King/Astral
    package, initializes every effective substat to zero, and calculates only
    after all required selections are complete.
  - **Outcome:** Corin or Lycaon begins from a complete deterministic setup and
    the existing Setup and Result experience remains intact.
  - **Covered by:** R1-R4, R8-R9, R14-R20, R27-R29
- F2. Corin contextual and selected pressure
  - **Trigger:** Dialyn supplies the retained recipient Ultimate opportunity,
    or an active broad pre-PEN effect changes Corin's effective inputs.
  - **Actors:** A1, A2
  - **Steps:** The Ultimate opportunity adds Puffer Electro 4-piece without
    selecting it. Broad pressure removes only its already-authored applicable
    PEN inputs; authorized preparation uses the pressure-safe first choice,
    while a direct pressure change clears an invalid edit without fallback.
  - **Outcome:** Candidate origin, prepared choice, and direct-edit lifecycle
    remain distinct and Result stays empty until any invalid selection is
    repaired.
  - **Covered by:** R7-R10, R24-R25, R29
- F3. Lycaon King pressure lifecycle
  - **Trigger:** The user selects, removes, or reselects King of the Summit
    4-piece on Lycaon.
  - **Actors:** A1, A2
  - **Steps:** King supplies the only current Lycaon CRIT threshold consumer.
    Its selection adds CRIT Rate Slot 4, CRIT Rate effective-substat hits, and
    Woodpecker Electro 2-piece. Removing King removes those memberships and
    clears invalid selections; reselecting King restores membership only.
  - **Outcome:** No fallback or old selection is restored, and Result follows
    the ordinary completeness gate.
  - **Covered by:** R15-R17, R29
- F4. King/Astral prepared allocation
  - **Trigger:** Lycaon is applied beside Dialyn or Trigger.
  - **Actors:** A1, A2
  - **Steps:** Preparation preserves competitive non-overlap: the holder with
    the established lower-opportunity-cost King fit keeps it, while Lycaon
    prepares Astral. Trigger's exact Additional qualification independently
    controls her current CRIT candidates; it does not rewrite the prepared
    holder allocation. Candidate membership does not change and direct edits
    remain local.
  - **Outcome:** The starting party uses King plus Astral without a runtime
    score, named-party lookup, null choice, or duplicate non-stacking holder.
  - **Covered by:** R18-R20
- F5. Current Result projection
  - **Trigger:** The applied party is complete.
  - **Actors:** A1, A2
  - **Steps:** Current Agent, Mindscape, Potential, equipment, party, recipient,
    Attribute, action, formula, threshold, and non-stacking meanings compose
    through the established surfaces.
  - **Outcome:** Result exposes only retained stats, modifiers, action
    differences, operations, sources, and gauges; raw damage, raw Daze, and
    rotations remain absent.
  - **Covered by:** R21-R29

## Requirements

### Admission, identity, and directions

- R1. Admit Corin and Lycaon beside the existing nine Agents. Corin is A-Rank,
  Physical, Attack, Victoria Housekeeping Co., and Focus-eligible. Lycaon is
  S-Rank, Ice, Stun, Victoria Housekeeping Co., and not Focus-eligible. Rank,
  Attribute, Specialty, faction, setup role, formula family, and Focus
  eligibility remain independent typed meanings.
- R2. Retain only completed level-60 values with a current setup or Result
  consumer. Corin retains ATK 807, CRIT Rate 5%, CRIT DMG 78.8%, and Energy
  Regen 1.2. Lycaon retains Impact 137, CRIT Rate 5%, and Energy Regen 1.2.
  Researched HP, DEF, Anomaly Mastery, Anomaly Proficiency, and other unused
  personal-damage bases do not enter product data merely because they are
  source facts.
- R3. Corin has no Potential Awakening and uses the generic A-Rank initial
  Mindscape M6. Lycaon uses generic S-Rank initial Mindscape M0 and completed
  Potential Awakening level 6. Potential remains completed progression rather
  than an input. Mindscape remains editable from M0 through M6 for both Agents.
- R4. Corin is a Physical crit-capable `general_damage` contributor centered on
  extended chainsaw actions, EX Special Attack, Chain Attack, and Ultimate.
  Lycaon is a Daze contributor and buffer centered on charged Basic Attack,
  Dodge Counter, Dash Attack, EX Special Attack, Defensive Assist, Assist
  Follow-Up, and the current Glacial Waltz operation. Lycaon has no retained
  personal-damage role; residual personal choices may occur only in variable
  main-stat slots when no role-strengthening stat exists there.
