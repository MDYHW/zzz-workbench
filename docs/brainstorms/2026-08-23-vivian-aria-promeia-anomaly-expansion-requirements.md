# Vivian, Aria, And Promeia Bounded Anomaly Expansion Requirements

## Summary

Add Vivian, Aria, and Promeia as the next rolling unit of the through-2.8
Anomaly damage/buildup track. The three Agents share Abloom as an exact visible
action outcome, but they consume it differently: Vivian is a primarily
off-field Ether Anomaly dealer, Aria is an on-field Ether Anomaly dealer, and
Promeia converts excess initial AM into personal AP and a squad Abloom bonus.

The permanent owners are `SF-001`-`SF-004`, `GV-001`-`GV-003` and
`GV-005`-`GV-009`, `FM-001`-`FM-004` and `FM-006`-`FM-011`,
`SW-002`-`SW-016`, and `UI-001`-`UI-004`. This requirement settles only the
bounded outcomes below. It does not create an Abloom damage formula, reaction
registry, or a rule for later Agents.

---

## Key Flows

- F1. Prepared Vivian, Aria, or Promeia
  - Party Apply and target-only rebuild use the existing candidate,
    representative, pressure, zero-count, and reconciliation lifecycle.
  - Each pool receives one complete deterministic package and only competitive
    editable alternatives.
- F2. Abloom Result
  - Existing stat, modifier, provider, operation, and action hierarchy
    relationships project Abloom-only differences without calculating base or
    final damage.
  - Parent Attribute Anomaly effects remain inherited by the Abloom child;
    Abloom-only effects do not leak to other anomaly outcomes.
- F3. Promeia AM conversion
  - The selected initial AM snapshot is evaluated once before ordinary provider
    delivery. Excess AM supplies personal AP and a squad Abloom bonus through
    one linear relationship, without feedback or a second derived pass.

## Requirements

**Vivian**

- R1. Add Vivian as S-Rank Ether Anomaly, Mockingbird, M0 by default, not
  Focus-eligible, with a primarily off-field operating interval. Completed
  level-60/full-Core values are ATK 880, AP 118, and AM 144. Her setup and
  Result formula families are `anomaly_damage` and `anomaly_buildup`.
- R2. Retain Vivian's Additional Ability as two all-party Fully Enabled anomaly
  modifier relationships: Corruption DMG +12%, and Disorder DMG +12% against
  an enemy suffering Corruption. The latter condition is source detail, not a
  recipient Attribute restriction. Exclude Feather, Flight Feather, cadence,
  duration, and base Abloom arithmetic.
- R3. Retain only Mindscape outcomes with current consumers. M1 gives all-party
  Attribute Anomaly and Disorder DMG +16% against a target under Prophecy. M2
  gives Vivian Ether buildup +25%, raises her Abloom source coefficient by
  130%, and lets Abloom ignore 15% RES. M4 gives ATK +12%. M6 gives Ether DMG
  +40%. Exclude guaranteed-CRIT, resource, and extra-Abloom base-result prose.
- R4. Full W-Engine candidates are Flight of Fancy W1, Angel in the Shell W1,
  and Weeping Gemini W5; non-limited offers Weeping. Prepare Flight in full and Weeping in
  non-limited. Flight's AP advanced stat, buildup, and reachable Ether-triggered
  AP package are fully usable off field. Angel retains its unconditional AP and
  AM in Vivian's off-field interval; its target DMG and Anomaly DMG clauses are
  removed off field and remain unused opportunity cost. This usable AP/AM
  package is materially stronger for Vivian's AP-scaled Abloom direction than
  Practiced Perfection: Practiced's ATK does not enter Abloom and its remaining
  AM does not establish a separate competitive direction. Practiced, Fusion
  Compiler, and Electro-Lip Gloss are legal or positive but rejected after
  whole-package comparison because their usable axes are covered more
  completely in the same pool without another materially distinct Vivian
  direction.
- R5. Phaethon's Melody is the prepared and admitted 4-piece direction. Its AP
  and other-holder EX Ether bonus are usable in Vivian's party interval.
  Prepare Freedom Blues as the distinct AP 2-piece; admit Chaos Jazz as its
  exact same-effect substitute. Chaotic Metal and Shining Aria are the same
  Ether 2-piece direction and expose only one identity at a time through the
  shared same-effect rule. Puffer Electro, Hormone Punk, and Astral Voice remain
  materially different PEN or ATK directions. Prepare AP/Ether DMG/AM in Slots 4/5/6; Slot 5 also
  admits PEN Ratio and ATK%. Effective substats are AP and ATK%.

**Aria**

- R6. Add Aria as S-Rank Ether Anomaly, Angels of Delusion, M0 by default,
  Focus-eligible, with an on-field operating interval. Completed level-60 base
  values are ATK 788, AP 116, and AM 115; completed Core adds AP +90 as an
  independent initial stat source. Her setup and Result formula families are
  `anomaly_damage` and `anomaly_buildup`.
- R7. Aria's Additional Ability qualifies with another Stun, Support, Anomaly,
  or Angels of Delusion Agent. Its resource and Corruption-duration clauses
  have no current Result consumer and remain invisible. Exclude Abloom base
  coefficients, stun-window base-result arithmetic, resources, and cadence.
- R8. Retain only Mindscape outcomes with current consumers. M1 gives Abloom
  CRIT Rate 25% and CRIT DMG 25%, plus 0.5% Abloom CRIT Rate per point of
  initial AM above 100, capped at 100%; Basic/Special/EX ignores 10% Ether
  buildup RES. M2 gives Aria's attacks and Abloom 24% DEF Ignore in the Fully
  Enabled Moment of Delusion snapshot. M6 gives enhanced Basic and Ultimate
  Ether DMG +40%. Exclude local-form resources and base-result multipliers.
- R9. Full W-Engine candidates are Angel in the Shell W1, Flight of Fancy W1,
  Electro-Lip Gloss W5, Weeping Gemini W5, and Fusion Compiler W1. Non-limited
  offers Electro, Weeping, and Fusion. Prepare Angel in full and Electro in
  non-limited. Angel's AM advanced stat, AP, on-field/Special activation,
  regular DMG, and Attribute Anomaly/Disorder bonus are all usable. Flight is a
  distinct AP/buildup package; the non-limited engines retain distinct stable,
  accumulating, or PEN/ATK packages.
- R10. Admit Phaethon's Melody and Shining Aria as competitive 4-piece
  directions and prepare Phaethon. Prepare Freedom Blues as the AP 2-piece;
  admit Chaos Jazz as its exact same-effect substitute and the applicable Ether,
  PEN, and ATK 2-piece directions. Shining Aria is the exposed Ether 2-piece
  while Phaethon is selected as 4-piece; Chaotic Metal replaces it when Shining
  itself is selected as 4-piece, so a legal role-swap never becomes a duplicate.
  Prepare AP/Ether DMG/AM in Slots 4/5/6;
  Slot 5 also admits ATK% and PEN Ratio. Effective substats are AP and ATK%.

**Promeia**

- R11. Add Promeia as S-Rank Ice Anomaly, Krampus, M0 by default,
  Focus-eligible, with an on-field operating interval. Completed level-60/full-
  Core values are ATK 872, AP 114, and AM 148. Her setup and Result formula
  families are `anomaly_damage` and `anomaly_buildup`.
- R12. Promeia's completed Core converts each point of initial AM above 150
  into personal AP +1.5 and all-party Abloom DMG +0.35%. Evaluate both outputs
  from the same selected initial-AM basis before ordinary provider delivery.
  This is linear; preserve the threshold in the relationship/source detail
  without inventing a cap, step function, or clamp family.
- R13. Her Additional Ability qualifies with another Anomaly or Support Agent.
  Retain Ice buildup +30%, Frostbite duration +3 seconds as a source-stated
  operation, and all-party Abloom DEF Ignore 40% against a target under
  Presumption. M1 raises that Abloom DEF Ignore to 60%. M2 gives AP +40 and
  adds 120% to the Abloom source coefficient. M6 gives her Attribute Anomaly
  and Disorder 15% RES Ignore. Exclude Embrace of Frost, resource, cadence, and
  special-Abloom base-result prose.
- R14. Full W-Engine candidates are Frostfall Sickle W1, Fusion Compiler W1,
  Angel in the Shell W1, Electro-Lip Gloss W5, and Weeping Gemini W5.
  Non-limited offers Fusion, Electro, and Weeping. Prepare Frostfall in full and
  Fusion in non-limited. Frostfall's AM, Ice DMG, and two-stack Abloom bonus are
  all usable. Fusion's PEN/ATK/Special-AP package remains the strongest distinct
  non-limited direction despite overlap with Promeia's DEF-ignore package.
- R15. Notes From the Chained is the prepared and admitted 4-piece direction.
  Its triggered AP and party Attribute Anomaly/Disorder bonus are usable.
  Prepare Phaethon's Melody as the AM 2-piece; admit Freedom Blues/Chaos Jazz
  AP and Hormone Punk/Astral Voice ATK as distinct alternatives. Prepare
  AP/Ice DMG/AM in Slots 4/5/6; Slot 5 also admits ATK% and PEN Ratio.
  Effective substats are AP and ATK%.

**Shared boundaries and lifecycle**

- R16. Add Abloom as the smallest exact action child required by current
  consumers. It inherits broader Attribute Anomaly effects, but Abloom-only
  modifiers, CRIT, DEF Ignore, RES Ignore, and provider effects remain exact.
  Corruption is likewise an exact action only where Vivian has a visible
  Corruption modifier. Neither creates a new formula family.
- R17. Flight of Fancy, Angel in the Shell, Frostfall Sickle, Phaethon's Melody
  4-piece, Shining Aria, and Notes From the Chained retain equipment-owned
  facts and compressed Setup copy separately. Agent profiles retain only
  holder/interval/applicability consequences and never copy those facts into
  Agent retained-value owners. Setup omits their routine trigger, stack,
  duration, and maintenance prose. Angel retains its anomaly-afflicted target
  scope and Shining Aria retains its stunned-target scope because those states
  define what the damage effect affects rather than how the package activates.
- R18. Party Apply prepares all three holders simultaneously. Pool or Mindscape
  rebuild prepares only the target using the other two established holder
  snapshots. Direct edits never reprepare; invalid choices clear without a
  fallback. Effective-substat counts initialize to zero before reconciliation,
  and incomplete selection keeps Result empty.
- R19. Tests extend shared action inheritance/exact-scope, linear-output,
  equipment, preparation, and visible interaction mechanisms. Do not add one
  catalogue suite per Agent or freeze researched source values solely because
  the identities are new.
- R20. Selected and candidate equipment keep the same compressed accessible
  descriptions and source interaction destinations. Add all three original
  portraits and verify desktop/narrow, expanded/compact geometry, baseline
  framing, readable names, control clearance, clipping, and horizontal
  overflow. Fine crop/alignment calibration remains deferred to the agreed
  post-expansion portrait batch and is not complete in this unit.

## Acceptance Examples

- AE1. Vivian's M1 appears on Attribute Anomaly and Disorder as
  `anomalyDmgBonus`; no `dmgTaken` row is created.
- AE2. An Abloom-only modifier appears on Abloom but not on Disorder or the
  Attribute Anomaly parent; an Attribute Anomaly modifier remains inherited by
  Abloom.
- AE3. Aria with Angel/Phaethon/Freedom/AP/Ether/AM exposes Angel's reachable
  package and M1 Abloom CRIT without a Jane-specific derivation.
- AE4. Promeia's initial AM of 248.64 produces AP +147.96 and squad Abloom DMG
  +34.524% from the same one-pass relationship. Her non-limited Fusion package
  reaches a different AM/AP/ATK/PEN direction without copied engine values.
- AE5. Replacing Promeia's selected W-Engine with an admitted package that
  supplies a different amount of AM changes both Core outputs after a direct
  edit; it does not reprepare any remaining Setup choice or restore edit
  history.
- AE6. A party without an Additional Ability qualifier omits only that
  relationship; one contrasting non-Anomaly Agent remains unchanged.
- AE7. Every prepared package is complete at zero effective-substat counts, and
  clearing any required choice empties Result until selection is restored.
- AE8. Hover and keyboard focus from an Abloom row reaches the exact Agent,
  Mindscape, W-Engine, or Disc source that supplied it.

## Out Of Scope

- Final anomaly damage, buildup progress, anomaly history, rotation, uptime,
  resources, cadence, and retained base coefficients.
- A general Abloom formula, reaction registry, new modifier type, second
  delivery/derived pass, runtime candidate scorer, rejected-item catalogue, or
  generic equipment compiler.
- Freedom Blues' deferred Attribute-scoped Result correction, Miyabi, and the
  separate Anton/Rina Shock-state general-damage track.
- Recalibrating already admitted Yanagi or Alice portraits in this unit.
