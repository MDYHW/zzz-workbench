# Drive Disc Candidate Corrections

## Summary

Apply the permanent
[Drive Disc Inspection Routing](../setup-workbench-product-contract.md#drive-disc-inspection-routing)
to current Disc candidates, then prove the correction through shared candidate,
preparation, different-set, and invalidation behavior. This document does not
own another inspection map.

---

## Ownership

The permanent product contract solely owns effect classification, inspection
order, formula and role routing, whole-package comparison, candidate-versus-
representative separation, and same-effect identity compression.

An existing vertical requirement owns its Agent-specific current outcome. The
following verticals have been corrected in place and are not restated here:

- [Soldier 0 vertical](2026-08-07-soldier-zero-vertical-requirements.md): Anby,
  Trigger, and Astra Disc outcomes;
- [Seed, Cissia, and Astra vertical](2026-08-09-seed-cissia-astra-vertical-requirements.md):
  Seed and Cissia Disc outcomes;
- [Evelyn vertical](2026-08-10-evelyn-vertical-requirements.md): Evelyn's Disc
  outcome;
- [Corin and Lycaon vertical](2026-08-12-corin-lycaon-vertical-requirements.md):
  Corin and Lycaon Disc outcomes; and
- [Yidhari and Manato vertical](2026-08-12-yidhari-manato-vertical-requirements.md):
  Yidhari and Manato Disc outcomes.

This document owns only the remaining bounded Agent outcomes below and the
shared implementation acceptance. If a later vertical becomes their current
owner, move the outcome there and remove it here rather than maintaining two
copies.

---

## Requirements

- R1. Implement the corrected current vertical outcomes through the existing
  candidate, preparation, different-set, pressure, and invalidation mechanisms.
  Do not create a numeric-equivalence registry, named-Agent rules table, runtime
  score, edited-substat optimizer, or second classification map.
- R2. Yixuan adds Chaotic Metal as a 2-piece candidate because matching Ether
  DMG modifies completed Sheer damage. It does not replace her authored
  zero-substat representative. She gains neither a standalone Max HP% 2-piece
  nor PEN Ratio 2-piece.
- R3. Dialyn exposes Swing Jazz as her one Energy Regen 2-piece identity because
  neither Swing Jazz nor Moonlight Lullaby has a current 4-piece role for her.
  Exact source facts remain distinct without presenting a duplicate choice.
- R4. Hugo retains Polar Metal. Selecting Hormone Punk 4-piece exposes Astral
  Voice as its legal ATK% complement; selecting another admitted 4-piece exposes
  Hormone Punk so its 4-piece role remains reachable.
- R5. Ju Fufu uses Hormone Punk as her one ATK% 2-piece identity because neither
  ATK% set has a current 4-piece role for her. Selecting Swing Jazz 4-piece
  exposes Moonlight Lullaby; selecting King of the Summit or Shockstar Disco
  exposes Swing Jazz.
- R6. Pan Yinhu selecting Astral Voice exposes Hormone Punk and Swing Jazz;
  selecting Swing Jazz exposes Astral Voice and Moonlight Lullaby; selecting
  Bunny in Wonderland exposes Astral Voice and Swing Jazz. Each relationship
  presents one same-effect member while preserving every current legal 4-piece
  role exchange.
- R7. A direct 4-piece edit reevaluates the displayed same-effect 2-piece
  identity. If the selected 2-piece is no longer offered or violates the
  different-set rule, clear it, leave Result incomplete, choose no substitute,
  and restore no history. Authorized preparation may later choose the authored
  complete package with every offered substat count initialized to zero.
- R8. Audit every current Agent through the permanent routing after the bounded
  corrections. Report any further missing or excessive candidate as a separate
  semantic unit; do not bulk-mutate it from similarity alone.

## Acceptance

- A1. Shared candidate coverage proves that a formula-valid matching Attribute
  2-piece is present for representative general-damage Attack and Rupture
  consumers, while standalone Max HP% and PEN Ratio remain absent from the
  contrasting Rupture consumer.
- A2. Shared same-effect coverage proves canonical display, legal role exchange,
  exact selected identity, different-set legality, source disclosure, and the
  direct-edit clear/incomplete/no-fallback/no-restoration lifecycle. It does not
  create one test suite per Agent.
- A3. Prepared-flow coverage proves that the added candidates do not replace an
  authored zero-substat first choice unless its current owning requirement says
  so, and that preparation never reacts to edited substat counts or Result.
- A4. The post-correction audit traces each proposed exception through exact
  consumer, formula/role route, holder and activation compatibility, nearest
  usable same-axis comparator, contrasting current case, complete package,
  finite opportunity cost, representative consequence, and lifecycle.

---

## Scope Boundaries

- No runtime Disc scoring, equipment recommendation, farming simulator,
  individual Disc inventory, edited-count feedback, or optimization.
- No exhaustive equipment catalogue, empty Attribute/action branches,
  composite `A OR B` artwork, or release-version field added only to settle a
  canonical tie.
- No automatic candidate mutation from the post-correction audit.
- Anomaly-specific membership remains outside this correction until its exact
  formula, buildup, Result, Attribute, and role consumers are bounded.
