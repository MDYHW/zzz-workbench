# Sunna And Nangong Yu Bounded Anomaly Expansion Requirements

## Summary

Add Sunna and Nangong Yu as the final paired production unit before the
Anomaly damage/buildup track-closing audit. Their dependency is a party and
setup relationship, not shared Specialty or identical personal output. Sunna
is a Physical Support whose retained ATK and Energy investment supplies party
buffs but no personal formula Result. Nangong is an Ether Stun Agent whose
hybrid setup materially strengthens anomaly damage, anomaly buildup, and Daze
while also supplying party and enemy-context effects.

The permanent owners are `SF-001`-`SF-004`, `GV-001`-`GV-003` and
`GV-005`-`GV-009`, `FM-001`-`FM-004` and `FM-006`-`FM-011`,
`SW-002`-`SW-016`, and `UI-001`-`UI-004`. This requirement settles only the
bounded Sunna/Nangong outcomes and the directly encountered shared equipment
projection corrections below. It does not authorize Miyabi, Anton/Rina, or
completion of the separate track-closing audit.

---

## Problem Frame

Specialty cannot answer either Agent's setup direction. Treating Sunna as an
Anomaly dealer would invent a personal Result consumer, while treating Nangong
as only a conventional Stun Agent would discard the AM, AP, and anomaly
buildup package that makes her setup competitive. Their retained relationships
must therefore reuse the existing Support and Stun profile owners while formula
participation, equipment competition, and visible projection remain
consumer-specific.

The equipment boundary also exposes two established translation risks. A
trigger action can activate a broad holder effect without becoming that
effect's action scope, and a holder may legally equip an engine while being
unable to activate one clause. Selected and candidate Setup copy is
source-owned: it compresses every materially distinct clause in the admitted
competitive package, including a clause that is unused by the current holder
when that clause is needed to disclose the complete package. An unused clause
contributes zero to candidate value and is not an opportunity-cost penalty.
Result alone filters the package to effects consumed by the current holder or
party.

## Key Flows

- F1. Prepared Sunna or Nangong
  - Party Apply and target-only rebuild use the existing candidate,
    representative, pressure, allocation, zero-count, and reconciliation
    lifecycle.
  - Each availability pool receives one complete deterministic package, and
    editable alternatives remain only when their whole usable package is
    competitive for that holder.
- F2. Sunna provider flow
  - Sunna's selected ATK/ER setup calculates her Initial ATK and Energy Regen.
    Qualified local relationships and ordinary delivery then project only the
    admitted party or enemy-context effects to compatible recipients.
  - Bubblegum, Claw Sharpeners, Cat's Gaze coefficients, healing, cadence, and
    final damage remain invisible.
- F3. Nangong hybrid flow
  - Nangong's selected Initial AM is evaluated once. AM above 110 supplies
    personal Impact linearly before ordinary provider delivery.
  - Her personal anomaly, buildup, and Daze relationships and her party or
    enemy-context effects project through the current canonical action,
    modifier, operation, and gauge mechanisms without a second derived pass.
- F4. Partial equipment package
  - Holder eligibility and clause activation are evaluated separately. A
    selected or candidate engine may retain a useful clause while Setup keeps
    the materially distinct whole package and Result omits a clause that the
    current holder cannot trigger or consume.
  - A trigger does not become the effect's action scope unless the retained
    equipment relationship says so.
