# Nekomata And Billy Vertical Requirements

## Summary

Admit Nekomata and Billy through the existing Party Setup and Result experience
using their current released behavior. Both are Physical Attack, Cunning Hares,
Focus-eligible, crit-capable `general_damage` contributors. Nekomata uses her
completed Potential Awakening, Dodge Counter and EX package, permanent back-
attack reachability, and Steel Cushion. Billy uses his Crouching Shot action
scope, Chain-to-Ultimate Additional Ability, broad Mindscape damage, and the
distinct Cloudcleave, Brimstone, Steel Cushion, and Starlight Engine Replica
packages.

The pair activates both exact Additional Ability conditions through shared
Attribute and faction. This is not a named duo rule. The vertical reuses current
general damage, action hierarchy, Seed Vanguard, broad pre-PEN pressure,
contextual Puffer, Focus-to-King, equipment, and responsive portrait meanings.
It adds no Purr Energy editor, Crouching-state simulator, Assault state,
distance or back-position validation, rotation, uptime score, raw damage,
runtime optimizer, or equipment catalogue.

## Product flows

- F1. Applying a Nekomata/Billy party prepares a complete pool-specific setup
  for each slot at zero supplied substats and populates Result only while all
  required selections remain complete.
- F2. Pool changes rebuild only the changed Agent. Direct equipment edits
  preserve unrelated inputs and project the chosen whole package through its
  exact usable and inactive clauses.
- F3. Nekomata and Billy enter the established Seed Vanguard comparison and
  crit-capable Focus-to-King preparation without changing either shared rule.
- F4. Dialyn adds contextual Puffer 4-piece membership. Broad pre-PEN pressure
  clears selected Puffer 2-piece and PEN mains without fallback or history;
  authorized party preparation authors a complete pressure-safe package.
- F5. Current Core, Potential, Additional, Mindscape, W-Engine, and Disc effects
  appear only on their exact headline or action Result surfaces. Both original
  portraits remain readable in desktop and narrow expanded/compact cards.

## Requirements

  identity and is not an input variant. Retain `general_damage` with ATK,

  Counter/EX consumers. Her retained completed level-60 inputs are ATK 910,
  CRIT Rate 19.4%, CRIT DMG 50%, and Energy Regen 1.2/s.
- R2. Admit Billy as A-Rank Physical Attack, Cunning Hares, Focus-eligible,
  rank-default M6. Retain `general_damage` with ATK, Physical/general DMG,
  CRIT, DEF/RES-region, and exact Basic, Dash, Dodge Counter, EX, Chain, and
  Ultimate consumers. His retained level-60 inputs are ATK 787, CRIT Rate
  19.4%, and CRIT DMG 50%.
- R3. Nekomata's Additional Ability is active when another applied Agent is
  Support or shares her Attribute or faction. Billy's is active when another
  applied Agent shares his Attribute or faction. Their pair activates both.
  Qualification changes only the exact action modifiers and never becomes a
  generic Physical or Cunning Hares party bonus.
- R4. Both Agents participate in the existing Seed Vanguard Initial-ATK
  comparison and qualify as crit-capable Focus Agents. Seed delivery and M2

## Rejected alternatives

- Do not model Purr Energy, Assault, Crouching, distance, enemy position,
  runtime stacks, or uptime. Fully Enabled retains only reachable current
  effects and exact action applicability.
- Do not admit every legal Attack W-Engine. Starlight loses the same-axis
  non-limited comparison to Brimstone, and Marcato loses the same-axis
  comparison to Sol; Nekomata cannot establish Replica's distance activation;
  inactive Ether/Electric/Fire clauses contribute zero rather than becoming
  invented Result effects or penalties.
- Do not admit Fanged Metal 4-piece for Nekomata by Attribute analogy or copy
  Billy's Shadow package. Current practice and defining actions settle different
  local 4-piece memberships.
- Do not add a generic resource, back-attack, distance, or named-party registry.
  Existing typed action/applicability clauses and one local pressure-safe
  prepared-package adjustment are sufficient for current consumers.
