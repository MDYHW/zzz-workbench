# Piper Anomaly Vertical Implementation Plan

## Summary

Extend the existing closed content, preparation, provider, calculation, Setup,
Result, and portrait seams to admit Piper without introducing an Anomaly
framework or simulator. Preserve Grace's local candidate-pressure exception
while giving Piper a complete Physical Anomaly setup in both W-Engine pools.

## Problem Frame

Grace proves that the workbench can project AP, AM, anomaly-damage, and
anomaly-buildup regions, but Piper introduces distinct current consumers:
Power as a visible capped basis, party-qualified regular DMG delivery, an
action-scoped Mindscape difference, and Physical complete-package choices.
The implementation must compose these through the existing three-Agent
lifecycle rather than copying Grace's equipment or generalizing her accepted
pre-PEN candidate pressure.

The prepared setup remains a deterministic editable start, not an optimizer.
The Result explains supplied stats, modifiers, thresholds, and source-owned
action differences; it does not compute Assault, Disorder, final damage,
application counts, rotations, or uptime.

## Requirements

- Implement R1-R20 and AE1-AE5 from the approved Piper requirements.
- Admit only Piper-consumed W-Engines and Disc packages, with independent full
  and non-limited representatives and exact selected/candidate compression.
- Preserve the permanent two-layer preparation order, zero initialization,
  rebuild scopes, invalid-selection clearing without fallback, and Empty
  Result behavior.
- Keep broad pre-PEN calculation regions applicable to Piper while limiting
  candidate-removal pressure to permanent `general_damage` consumers and the
  already accepted Grace-local exception.
- Verify portrait source and framing in the running application at desktop and
  narrow widths, expanded and compact, before closure.

## Key Technical Decisions

1. Extend the existing exhaustive typed maps and Agent-local calculator. Do not
   add a registry, generic Anomaly projector, new preparation pass, or provider
   phase for a single new consumer.
2. Add `Power` to the current Result metric vocabulary because Piper has a
   source-owned visible current/cap and qualification-only threshold gauge.
   Keep Core buildup and Additional Ability DMG as separate contributions.
3. Split candidate-pressure eligibility from formula-region eligibility.
   Candidate removal accepts `general_damage` plus exact Grace; calculation
   delivery continues to recognize both current damage formula families.
4. Reuse the existing action hierarchy for Piper M2 and suppress Assault and
   Disorder rows when no selected source differs from the parent anomaly
   region.
5. Extend shared mechanism tests with Piper only where she creates a distinct
   failure mode. Retained numeric facts stay in content; tests prove reference
   integrity, composition, lifecycle, and visible behavior rather than acting
   as a second fact catalogue.

## Rejected Alternatives

- **Copy Grace's candidate behavior.** Rejected because Grace's anomaly
  pre-PEN pressure is a bounded local outcome and SW-007 does not authorize a
  generic Anomaly extension.
- **Add all dormant Anomaly equipment.** Rejected because unused facts and
  assets have no Piper consumer and would create a catalogue.
- **Create a shared Anomaly abstraction now.** Rejected because one Electric
  and one Physical Agent do not establish a stable common interface beyond the
  existing formula and provider seams.
- **Render generic Assault, Disorder, M4 event-Energy, duration, or final-
  damage rows.** Rejected because those clauses do not create distinct
  qualifying current outcomes and the service is not a simulator. Piper's
  retained ordinary Energy Regen stat still follows the existing stat row.
- **Treat zero prepared substats as no investment opportunity.** Rejected
  because zero is initialization while AP and ATK remain finite editable
  opportunities authored by the selected package.

## Scope Boundaries

- No final Assault, Disorder, anomaly-damage, or buildup arithmetic.
- No rotation, probability, application-history, remaining-time, field-time,
  trigger-frequency, or uptime model.
- No admission of another Agent, post-2.8 content, unused Disc set, or unused
  W-Engine.
- No generic Anomaly abstraction, optimizer, provider phase, preparation pass,
  evidence registry, compatibility layer, or unrelated cleanup.
- No change to the permanent authority meaning; the implementation remains
  subordinate to the approved Piper-local outcome.

## Risks And Mitigations

- **Pressure drift:** a shared helper currently spans both damage formula
  families. Split only candidate membership and preserve calculation delivery;
  prove Piper and Grace together.
- **Source conflation:** Power, Core buildup, and Additional Ability can look
  like one gauge. Keep their sources and outputs separate in types, projection,
  and source-link tests.
- **Equipment-copy drift:** structured facts, selected copy, candidate copy,
  and Result projection can diverge. Reuse one content fact and assert both
  visible equipment surfaces plus the consumer-specific Result.
- **Lifecycle regression:** targeted rebuilds can accidentally reset unrelated
  Agents. Assert object/input preservation and zeroing boundaries in composed
  party flows.
- **Portrait false confidence:** local asset presence and DOM wiring do not
  establish framing. Inspect the source and complete the required browser
  matrix before commit.
