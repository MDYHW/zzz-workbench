# Jane Doe And Seth Lowell Bounded Anomaly Expansion

## Outcome

Add Jane Doe and Seth Lowell through the current shared setup and calculation
harness. Each availability pool receives a deterministic complete starting
setup, editable competitive candidates, and only the admitted Result values,
action differences, operations, gauges, and source destinations owned by the
approved requirement and permanent authorities.

The visible flow must keep Jane's Physical Anomaly damage direction and
Assault-only outcomes distinct from Seth's off-field provider direction while
preserving the existing party, target-rebuild, direct-edit, completeness, and
source-focus behavior.

## Execution Units

1. **Close inputs before editing.** Verify the exact clean main checkpoint,
   reread the permanent owners, approved Jane/Seth requirement, current shared
   consumers, and applicable workflow learnings. Resolve the missing shared
   Peacekeeper fact from an official current source before admitting Seth, and
   keep that equipment meaning out of Agent-local content.
2. **Add settled setup content.** Add Jane and Seth identity, retained values,
   candidates, pool representatives, setup formula participation, main-stat and
   effective-substat choices, equipment references, provider direction, and
   allocation metadata through the existing content owners. Review this unit
   before calculation work.
3. **Add bounded calculation meaning.** Express Jane and Seth through the
   existing Anomaly and provider-defense profiles. Apply ordinary delivery,
   derive Jane's Passion ATK and Assault CRIT once from completed Fully Enabled
   AP, apply derived delivery once, and then compose final recipients. Preserve
   Seth's exact provider and action scopes without recursion, feedback, or a new
   generic formula layer.
4. **Preserve composed lifecycle behavior.** Integrate contextual pressure and
   shared Disc allocation without changing the established order. Party Apply
   rebuilds all three setups; pool or Mindscape changes rebuild only the target
   against established holders; direct edits do not prepare. Invalid selections
   clear without fallback or history, zero supplied counts remain initialized,
   and incomplete selections keep Result empty.
5. **Close visible assets and verification.** Inspect the original Jane and Seth
   portraits, author only the shared source metadata that survives rendered
   comparison, and verify desktop and narrow expanded/compact destinations.
   Run shared mechanism and representative-flow tests, type check, production
   build, diff checks, and final controller review before plan closure.

## Ownership And Dependencies

- The five permanent Markdown owners define formula, vocabulary, retention,
  setup/lifecycle, Result, and portrait meaning. The approved Jane/Seth
  requirement owns only the bounded local outcomes.
- The controller owns candidate and representative admission, recipient and
  action scope, formula ordering, lifecycle meaning, visual acceptance, final
  diff review, and external transactions.
- Read-only source or symbol inventory may be delegated at a lower route. A
  settled content or test unit may be delegated only with exact owned files and
  acceptance, followed by controller inspection. A worker stops on a missing
  owner, unsupported candidate, new common mechanism, or visible consequence
  outside the closed unit.
- The shared Peacekeeper fact is a blocking dependency for Seth content. The
  original portrait assets and live in-app Browser are blocking dependencies
  for portrait closure.

## Behavior Verification

- A Jane/Seth representative journey proves ordinary AP delivery, the single
  Jane derivation, recipient composition, and the admitted action projection.
- A Seth qualification contrast proves that the provider and enemy-context
  clause reach only their admitted recipients and formula consumers.
- A composed lifecycle journey covers Party Apply, target-only pool/Mindscape
  rebuild, direct edit, selected pressure present/absent/reselected, invalid
  clearing without fallback, zero supplied-count initialization, and incomplete
  Result while preserving an unaffected contrasting consumer.
- Candidate and equipment tests remain reference- and mechanism-oriented. They
  do not freeze Jane/Seth rosters, copied equipment numbers, or prepared outcomes
  as shared invariants.
- Browser verification covers a real Jane/Seth party and Result interaction at
  desktop and narrow widths, with both portraits expanded and compact. Check
  readable names, face and connected-body balance, control clearance, clipping,
  horizontal overflow, source interaction, and console state.

## Non-Goals

- No generic Anomaly CRIT model, final anomaly-damage calculator, rotation or
  uptime optimizer, shield UI, survival Result, or runtime candidate scorer.
- No recursive provider graph, formula DSL, new generic post-delivery hook, or
  compatibility path beside the current shared harness.
- No Freedom Blues Result projection in this bounded vertical.
- No Seth personal-damage direction, personal effective-substat family, or
  personal Disc Result created from a positive local clause alone.
- No Agent-local copies of W-Engine or Drive Disc facts, activation clauses, or
  simple equipment-derived totals.
- No automatic selection fallback, edit-history restoration, Agent-specific
  catalogue suite, unrelated UI redesign, or later expansion-cohort Agent.

## Rejected Alternatives

- **Treat current implementation or tests as the semantic baseline:** rejected
  because permanent owners and current consumers must independently support the
  local outcome before fidelity checks have meaning.
- **Add an Agent calculator or provider switch:** rejected because Jane and Seth
  fit the current profile, relationship, delivery, and projection harness except
  for the already-bounded one-way Jane derivation.
- **Copy equipment values into Jane or Seth content:** rejected because equipment
  owns its condition and effect while the holder owns only eligibility,
  activation compatibility, and candidate admission.
- **Test every Jane/Seth outcome by name:** rejected because Agent identity or a
  different numeric threshold does not create a new mechanism.
