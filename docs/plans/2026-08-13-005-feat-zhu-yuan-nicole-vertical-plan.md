  complete pressure-aware setups; clear-without-fallback remains a direct-edit

  Elegant, Vault, Chaotic, Moonlight, and Astral retain separate eligibility,

  the current applied Additional/M6 context and the bounded future opportunity;
  later direct Mindscape edits preserve unrelated setup inputs.

  rewrite every existing representative. Adjust only current general-damage
  packages whose prepared PEN or standalone Puffer input becomes invalid.
- Do not add Anomaly Result, rotations, resource cadence, raw damage, historical
  versions, evidence payloads, source registries, named-party rules, or per-Agent
  UI/test suites. External research remains ephemeral.
